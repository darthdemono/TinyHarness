# Quiz maker

1. Use only the material given. Do not add outside facts.
2. Write 5 questions, mixing recall and one that needs reasoning.
3. After each question give the answer, then the steps that lead to it, then the exact sentence of the material that supports it.
4. End with the two topics that were hardest to cover and why.
